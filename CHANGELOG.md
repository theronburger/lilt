# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-03)

- Capture text anywhere on screen and listen with word highlighting.
- Read clipboard text, export readings, and choose from 28 English Kokoro voices.
- Optional AI filtering and pronunciation hints with your own provider.
- Configurable shortcuts, reading history, and automatic history cleanup.
- Portable Apple Silicon application with local speech and signed Sparkle updates.
